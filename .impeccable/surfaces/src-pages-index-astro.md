**Scope:** Home page only. **Visitor mode:** Persuade.

**Audience & job:** UAE construction / oil & gas / power-gen / industrial buyers and their procurement staff, comparing suppliers and wanting a fast, specific quote — with a clear Buy-vs-Hire intent for the same catalog.

**Action:** get a quote (Request-a-Quote / per-category enquiry) or reach Collins by WhatsApp/Call; understand the range and that Collins is a trustworthy local operator.

**Proof / content on this surface:** equipment categories with real spec ranges (Generators 10–1250 KVA, Forklifts 1.4–15 t, Air Compressors 135–1050 CFM, Heavy Equipment, Tower Lights, Spare Parts); dual buy+rent; two UAE branches; founder-led trust (Owner Message); top brands (Perkins, Kalmar, Deutz); on-site stats 4,000+/3+ yrs/50+ (VERIFY before featuring). Keep Collins logo unaltered.

**Chosen direction — "The Working Drawing"** (seed 08c5912a): the catalog rendered as an engineer's dimensioned working drawings on a Collins-navy blueprint ground; white technical linework, dimension callouts, title blocks, buy/hire stamps. Orange for primary actions, one safety-yellow accent (SP continuity), Collins blue in the logo only. Refuses SP's sunset-silhouette + safety-yellow-caps rut while keeping the industrial-trust register. Condensed engineered caps display · mono labels/numerals · humanist body.

**Memorable moment:** dimension lines draw on as sections reveal; a buy/hire toggle flips the title-block stamp and pre-fills enquiry intent.

**Kept warm (anti-risk):** founder Owner Message + one real dusk jobsite photo so the blueprint never reads as a sterile CAD demo.

**Unresolved:** 2nd branch address, social URLs (only Instagram confirmed), catalog PDF lead magnet, on-site stats verification. Palette scope resolved toward the navy-blueprint world (not full SP black/yellow; not the plain-navy incumbent).
